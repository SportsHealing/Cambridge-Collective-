Purpose

This policy explains how I keep students safe during tuition. It applies to every session, message and piece of work.

Who is responsible

Louisa Grinyer is the sole tutor and the person responsible for safeguarding. I hold an enhanced DBS check. The certificate is available for parents, guardians and schools to see on request. [DBS certificate date and update service status.]

How sessions run

  • Sessions take place online on [Zoom], using a link I send to the parent, guardian or school.
  • A parent or guardian is welcome to sit in on any session.
  • Students should join from a shared or open space at home, not a bedroom, where possible.
  • Sessions are not recorded unless a parent, guardian or school asks and agrees in writing.
  • I keep a short record of each session: date, topic and work set.

Communication

  • For students under 18, I arrange sessions through a parent, guardian or school email.
  • Students may email me about work, with a parent, guardian or school copied in.
  • I do not contact students through social media or personal messaging apps.
  • Students aged 18 or over may contact me directly.

Conduct

  • I treat every student with respect and expect the same in return.
  • I do not meet students in person without a parent, guardian or school staff member present. [Confirm whether in-person sessions are offered.]
  • I keep professional boundaries in every session and message.

If I have a concern

If I see or hear something that worries me, I will write it down the same day. I will then speak to the parent or guardian, or the school's designated safeguarding lead. If a child is in immediate danger, I will call the police on 999. I may also contact the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000 for advice. I keep these records securely and separate from teaching notes.

If you have a concern

Email louisagrinyer7@gmail.com. Parents, guardians and students can also contact the NSPCC on 0808 800 5000, or Childline on 0800 1111.
