Safeguarding policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Purpose

This policy explains how students are kept safe during tuition. It applies to every session, message and piece of work.

Who is responsible

Louisa Grinyer is responsible for the safeguarding of students. All tutors hold enhanced DBS checks. These certificates are available to parents, guardians and schools on request.

How sessions run

  • Sessions take place on Zoom or Google Meet, depending on the student's preference. The link goes to the parent, guardian or school.
  • A parent or guardian is welcome to sit in on any session.
  • Students should join from a shared or open space at home, where possible.
  • Sessions are not recorded unless a parent, guardian or school asks and agrees in writing.
  • Neither photos nor screenshots of the students or the tutors will be taken under any circumstances during the call.
  • Any concern during the lesson will be reported to Louisa (louisa@cambridgecollective.co.uk) to be investigated and resolved.

Communication

  • For students under 18, sessions are arranged through a parent, guardian or school email.
  • Students may email Louisa at louisa@cambridgecollective.co.uk about work, with a parent, guardian or school copied in.
  • Students aged 18 or over may get in touch directly, using the email address above.
  • Tutors and students will not engage in any discussions not related to the work on any messaging or social media sites.

Conduct

  • All students and tutors are treated equally, regardless of their background. Any concerns about a tutor's conduct can be reported to louisa@cambridgecollective.co.uk.
  • Professional boundaries are kept in every session and message.

If a tutor has a concern

Any concern about a student is recorded the same day. It is shared with the parent, guardian or the school's safeguarding lead.

If you have a concern

Please email louisa@cambridgecollective.co.uk.

Data

Personal data is handled as set out in the privacy policy.
