Privacy policy

Who I am

Cambridge Collective is run by Louisa Grinyer, a sole trader based in London, UK. I am the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact me at louisagrinyer7@gmail.com.

What this website collects

Nothing. The site has no forms, no cookies and no analytics. Every button and contact link opens your own email program.

The fonts load from Google Fonts. Google may record your IP address when a font is requested. See Google's privacy policy.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses for security. See GitHub's note on data collection.

What is collected when you contact us

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • The student's name, year group and school, where you share them.
  • Anything you tell us about learning needs, goals or timing.
  • Work the student sends, such as essays or personal statement drafts.
  • Invoices and payment records.

For students under 18, a parent, guardian or school should make contact and share this information.

Why it is used and the legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a free call. Basis: steps you ask for before a contract.
  • To plan, deliver and review tuition. Basis: performance of a contract.
  • To invoice and keep accounts. Basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep students safe, in line with the safeguarding policy. Basis: legitimate interests and legal obligation.

Cambridge Collective does not send marketing emails, or sell or share data for advertising.

Who else sees your data

  • Google, which provides our email account and Google Meet.
  • Zoom, which hosts sessions when a student prefers it.
  • Microsoft, which provides Word online for shared documents.

Some of these providers store data outside the UK. They do so under contract terms the UK recognises as adequate protection.

How long is this information kept?

  • Enquiries that do not lead to tuition: deleted after three months.
  • Student records and work: deleted six months after tuition ends.
  • Invoices and payment records: kept for at least five years after the tax return deadline, as HMRC requires.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask me to:

  • show you the data I hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it, where I have no legal duty to keep it;
  • limit how I use it;
  • give you a copy in a usable format;
  • stop using it where I rely on legitimate interests.

Email louisagrinyer7@gmail.com. I will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with my answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
