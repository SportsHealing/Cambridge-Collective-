Services

Support at every stage

Oxbridge admissions

Personal statements, admissions tests and mock interviews, led by Oxbridge graduates.

Best for: students aged 15 or over looking for dedicated academic support

Subject-specific tuition

One-to-one 11+, GCSE and A Level support, focused on exam technique, essay skills and fortifying knowledge.

11+ preparation covers independent school entrance exams, ISEB tests and school-specific assessments.

Exam boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Eduqas and Cambridge International (IGCSE).

Best for: Years 4 to 13 wanting focused support

Reading groups

Small group sessions that build critical reading and analytical writing through modern and classical texts. Recent texts include War Horse, The Hate You Give and Animal Farm.

Best for: KS2 to KS4 (ages 7 to 16), in groups of 2 to 6

Also available: personal statements for other UK universities, Extended Project (EPQ) support and essay competition coaching.

FAQ

Common questions

Which year groups do you teach?

Years 4 to 13. Oxbridge preparation is for students aged 15 and over.

How do online sessions work?

You join a video call from a laptop or tablet. A quiet space works best.

How long is a session?

60 or 90 minutes, depending on preference.

What happens in the free call?

We talk for 20 minutes about goals, current level and timing. There is no obligation to book.

How do I pay?

Sessions are invoiced monthly and paid by bank transfer. Packages are paid upfront. Email louisa@cambridgecollective.co.uk with any questions.

What if I need to cancel?

There is a 24-hour cancellation policy. Late cancellations may be charged in full. Email louisa@cambridgecollective.co.uk if you need to cancel.

Safeguarding

Students come first

All tutors hold enhanced DBS checks. Certificates are available on request. Every session follows the safeguarding policy.
