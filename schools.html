What a session looks like

  • Group size: up to 15 students for workshops, up to 10 for reading groups.
  • Length: 90 minutes to 2 hours for workshops, 90 minutes for reading groups.
  • Before the session: an outline of the session, a reading list and a briefing sheet for students.
  • After the session: written feedback on each student within a week, with follow-up tasks for each one.
  • Pricing: per session.
